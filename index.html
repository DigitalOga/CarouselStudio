Carousel Studio: free social media carousel maker

Carousel Studio is a free, installable carousel maker for Instagram, LinkedIn and other social platforms. Create swipeable slides, set up your brand colours and fonts once, and your carousels autosave on your device. It works in the browser and installs as an app on Android and iPhone.

Carousel Studio was designed and built by Olaleye Abayomi, also known as DigitalOga, an AI trainer, product builder and author based in Lagos, Nigeria, under Lumynaire and Brandglam Media.
